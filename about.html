I am Samuel HUANG 黃同榮, a graduate student in architecture. My studies and practice explore how architecture can connect local context, public life, and digital methods.

01 / PROFILE

Profile

I work through architectural design and explore how space can respond to local needs. Model making, digital tools, and collaboration help turn ideas into spatial proposals that can be discussed and experienced.

Building with a Bunun community in Taitung made me attentive to collective making, site conditions, and participation. Recent design research extends these interests to public space and interactive façades.
